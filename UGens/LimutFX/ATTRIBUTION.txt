Limut-inspired effects for Dexter U.S. FoxDot

The PingPong and Duck effect behavior is adapted from the effect definitions
named "pingpong" and "duck" in:
  Limut, by sdclibbery
  https://github.com/sdclibbery/limut/blob/main/lib/effects.limut

The Compressor control model and Vocoder node topology are adapted from Limut:
  https://github.com/sdclibbery/limut/blob/main/lib/effects.limut
  https://github.com/sdclibbery/limut/blob/main/lib/nodes.limut
