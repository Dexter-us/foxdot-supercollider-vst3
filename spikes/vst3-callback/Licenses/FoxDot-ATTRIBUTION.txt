FoxDot compatibility attribution

This plugin includes an initial compatibility layer based on the FoxDot live-coding interface and sound definitions. FoxDot is available at https://github.com/Qirky/FoxDot.

This release also adapts FoxDot's band-pass `bpnoise` modulation and envelope-driven `swell` effect behavior from `FoxDot/lib/Effects/Util.py`, `FoxDot/osc/sceffects/bandPassFilter.scd`, and `FoxDot/osc/sceffects/filterSwell.scd`. The implementations here are translated for the plugin's embedded renderer and do not use OSC or an external SuperCollider server.

FoxDot-derived adaptation in this plugin is distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license (CC BY-SA 4.0). The complete license text is included as FoxDot-CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt.

This plugin does not bundle FoxDot's OSC server, external clock thread, or sample library. The compatibility layer translates supported player/tempo expressions to the plugin's embedded SuperCollider engine.
