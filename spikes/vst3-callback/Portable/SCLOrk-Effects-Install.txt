SCLOrk-derived stereo insert pack
================================

This pack contains three precompiled SuperCollider SynthDefs, not three new
SCPlugins .scx modules. The definitions use SuperCollider core UGens already
included in the plugin, so there is no separate UGen DLL or SuperCollider
rebuild to install.

IMPORTANT: these SynthDef names are recognized by the updated Dexter engine
in version 0.4.161. A 0.4.160 plugin will not expose them in the compatibility
checker or Effects panel. Wait for/install the matching 0.4.161-or-newer VST3
before using these files.

After installing the matching VST3:
1. Close the DAW completely.
2. Extract this ZIP into the existing dexter_sc_tone.vst3 folder and merge its
   Portable and Licenses folders. Do not delete or replace the existing
   Portable/Samples, Portable/SCPlugins, or personal SynthDefs.
3. Restart the DAW. The three loaded names should appear under Effects:
   SCLOrkPhazerFX, SCLOrkDecimatorFX, and SCLOrkCrossoverDistortionFX.
4. Open Portable/201-sclork-insert-effects.py in the plugin editor, or use the
   same effect names and controls on a player line.

Controls and ranges
-------------------
SCLOrkPhazerFX:
  fx_mix 0..1, fx_rate 0.05..8 Hz, fx_depth 0..1,
  fx_center 150..1800 Hz, fx_spread 0..1, fx_q 0.05..1,
  fx_tone 200..16000 Hz.

SCLOrkDecimatorFX:
  fx_mix 0..1, fx_rate 80..22000 Hz, fx_bits 1..16,
  fx_drive 0.5..8, fx_tone 200..16000 Hz.

SCLOrkCrossoverDistortionFX:
  fx_mix 0..1, fx_drive 0.5..8, fx_shape 0..1,
  fx_tone 200..16000 Hz.

The DSP smooths control changes. The rate/tone ceiling is also limited safely
against the active sample rate. Source attribution and GPL-3.0 license are
included with the SynthDefs. See Portable/SynthDefs/README.txt.
