SCLOrk-derived player effects
=============================

These three stereo insert adaptations are based on SCLOrkSynths by Josh
Mitchell, licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3.0:

  SCLOrkSynths/SynthDefs/misc/phazer.scd
  SCLOrkSynths/SynthDefs/misc/decimator.scd
  SCLOrkSynths/SynthDefs/misc/crossoverDistortion.scd

Upstream project: https://github.com/SCLOrkHub/SCLOrkSynths
The corresponding full GPL-3.0 license text is in
Licenses/SCLOrkSynths-GPL-3.0.txt.

The adaptations replace each voice's oscillator/envelope with the VST's
stereo player-effect input bus. The three effects are named
SCLOrkPhazerFX, SCLOrkDecimatorFX, and SCLOrkCrossoverDistortionFX. They use
SuperCollider core UGens and do not require an additional UGen DLL.
